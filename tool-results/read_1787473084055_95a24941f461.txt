     8→     8→## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
     9→     9→
    10→    10→**Key Finding**: **No competitor currently offers multi-method side-by-side comparison that includes PAPSS as a payment rail.** This is a significant market gap. The closest is Monito, which compares remittance providers (Wise, Remitly, Western Union, etc.) but does not include PAPSS, Swift, or correspondent banking as comparison options. FX transparency varies wildly — Wise leads with mid-market rate + transparent fee, while SendWave, Remitly, and Flutterwave embed markups in their exchange rates.
    11→    11→
    12→    12→**PAPSS/AfCFTA Mentions**: Zero of the 11 competitors explicitly mention PAPSS or AfCFTA on their consumer-facing platforms. PAPSS is mentioned only by central banks (CBN mandated adoption in Nigeria) and in academic/policy papers. The $5B+ annual savings from PAPSS remains completely untapped by consumer/comparison tools.

   230→   230→- PAPSS is a cross-border payment infrastructure connecting 80+ Pesalink participants to 160+ banks and fintechs across Africa
   231→   231→- Could save **over $5 billion annually** in fees, FX spreads, and delays
   232→   232→- CBN (Nigeria) has mandated all banks to adopt PAPSS
   233→   233→- Enables real-time, low-cost cross-border payments in local currencies
   234→   234→- Eliminates correspondent banking fees

   236→   236→- Regulatory barriers identified in Rwanda, Ghana, Kenya, Nigeria are slowing uptake

   289→   289→## PART 3: FX COMPARISON TOOL BENCHMARKS (How Monito, Wise Compare Work)
   290→   290→
   291→   291→### How FX Comparison Tools Generally Work
   292→   292→
   293→   293→1. **User Input**: User enters send country/currency, receive country/currency, and amount
   294→   294→2. **Data Collection**: Engine queries APIs/integrations of 20-50+ money transfer providers in real-time for that specific corridor
   295→   295→3. **Rate Calculation**: For each provider, calculates: (a) transfer fee, (b) exchange rate applied, (c) FX markup vs. mid-market rate, (d) total recipient amount, (e) estimated delivery speed
   296→   296→4. **Ranking & Display**: Results are ranked by "best value" (most money received), displayed in a table or list with key metrics side-by-side
   297→   297→5. **Monito Score**: Monito adds a quality/reliability score factoring in: regulatory licenses, customer reviews, complaint history, and service quality
   298→   298→6. **Monetization**: Affiliate model -- earns referral commission when users click through to a provider and complete transfer
